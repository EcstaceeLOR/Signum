## Signum's mechanic fingerprint

Signum combines these properties:

1. The player authors a **complete ordered binary rhythm** of Tap and Rest beats.
2. After commitment, VRF produces an independent complete binary ghost signal of equal length.
3. Every position is compared by equality and the **Hamming similarity count** selects a fixed payout tier.
4. Receiver length changes the binomial distribution and paytable while every authored pattern remains equiprobable.
5. The result is staged as an audiovisual call-and-response performance, not a number draw or reel spin.
6. It is one wager and one settlement with no cash-out timing or hidden intermediate state.

The novelty is the combination of authored rhythm, complete signal-versus-signal comparison, positional match scoring, and call-and-response reveal. “Matching random things” by itself is not novel.

## Novelty strengths

- The player creates the object that appears throughout commitment, reveal, result, history, and sound.
- A single rule supports three mathematically transparent volatility profiles.
- Every beat can be heard and seen, making the random comparison legible rather than decorative.

- The fairness proof can reconstruct the exact result with XOR and popcount, which is unusually teachable.

## Presentation guardrails

Signum should look and sound like a signal instrument:

- two aligned waveform rails labelled `YOURS` and `CHAIN ECHO`;
- Tap as a short upward impulse and Rest as a quiet gap, never heads/tails;
- beat-by-beat tones with a consonant match sound and a muted miss sound;
- receiver hardware language, not number-grid, reel, wheel, or minefield language;
- a perfect match resolves as harmonic convergence, not a slot jackpot animation.

It must not use:

- Keno balls, numbered tiles, spot counts, draw cages, or “hits”;
- coin faces, flip animation, parlay/leg language;
- reels, paylines, wilds, scatters, or cascading symbols;
- radar tuning, hidden-frequency search, or proximity hints;
- copy suggesting a pattern can beat or predict the random signal.
